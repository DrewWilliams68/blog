### Valueable Skills and Talents
I believe clear communication and open-mindedness are of the utmost importance when doing social change work with a community partner. You need to be able to clearly articulate your plans and strategies in order for you and your community partner to be on the same page. If this doesn't happen, making a complete result where both parties are happy becomes a lot more difficult. I believe you also have to be open-minded because your community partner's wants are bound to change based on the initial work you do, or if the requirements become clearer. I feel like my communication is good, but I don't always come off as clearly as I would like. I also feel like I could have been more receptive of our community partner's wants. When we met with our community partner, I felt like some of the alterations were different that what I would have wanted, and I didn't fully implement everything that was asked. But I feel like I have improved my communication skills throughout this project and it is a great glimpse of how my future jobs will look. 

### Social Change
Considering the social needs of this campus community, making donations and projects easier to access and contribute to definely are effective ways of creating change in this area. Hendrix is very good at fostering opportunities and maintaining there great facitilies. If we make it easier for gifts to be donated to specific projects, Hendrix would be able to allocate funds to more projects and provide more opportunities to Hendrix students, faculty, and community members. Overall, I believe this project is successful in fostering social change within our Hendrix community.

### How Do I Feel About My Contributions?
Overall, I feel very positive about this experience and my work during this project. Hendrix has always provided me with the opportunity to grow and improve myself. This project has allowed me to actually give back and leave a part of my work behind. This project was part of the Database and Websystems curriculum, and it allowed me to be able to contribute a useful website to Hendrix. I feel like I have also been able to make a difference within the community. I have allowed for doners to be able to contribute funds in an easier way as well as pick exactly what their funding goes too. These changes should increase the amount of donations we get from people who want to know where their money goes, which will allow for more students and community members to enjoy the facilities and programs the funding contributes to. This project was very positive and I enjoyed working with my peers and Mr. Turner.

### Issues
This experience has reshaped my social views of private universities. Before and during my time here at Hendrix, I thought it was like most other private universities. They typically have better education and have better opportunities with the drawbacks of being very expensive, and I wasn't sure if the price will ever be worth it. Tuition is so expensive, but im contributing to so many opportunities for all students and community members. If we didn't have these cruical donations, tuition would be more expensive and Hendrix wouldn't be as accessable. I have also gained a deeper understanding of why we should support the institutions we enjoy, because without a community supporting the institution, we wouldn't have the opportunities we enjoy. Donations are a crucial part of Hendrix's success, so I feel lucky to be able to make the process of donating a lot easier!
