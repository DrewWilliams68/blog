### Valueable Skills and Talents
I believe clear communication and open-mindedness are of the utmost importance when doing social change work with a community partner. You need to be able to clearly articulate your plans and strategies in order for you and your community partner to be on the same page. If this doesn't happen, making a complete result where both parties are happy becomes a lot more difficult. I believe you also have to be open-minded because your community partner's wants are bound to change based on the initial work you do, or if the requirements become clearer. I feel like my communication is good, but I don't always come off as clearly as I would like. I also feel like I could have been more receptive of our community partner's wants. When we met with our community partner, I felt like some of the alterations were different that what I would have wanted, and I didn't fully implement everything that was asked.

### Social Change
Considering the social needs of this campus community, making donations and projects easier to access and contribute to definely are effective ways of creating change in this area. Hendrix is very good at fostering opportunities and maintain there great facitilies. If we make it easier for gifts to be donated to specific projects, hendrix would be able to allocate funds to more projects and provide more opportunities to hendrix students, faculty, and community members.

### Fundamental values, social ideas, and significant beliefs
I feel like my social ideals have been confirmed throughout this process. Hendrix has always provided me with the opportunity to grow and improve myself. This project has allowed me to actually give back and leave a part of my work behind. This project was part of the Database and Websystems curriculum, and it allowed me to be able to contribute a useful website to Hendrix. I feel like Computer Science coursework has also challenged my beliefs. I often have wondered how money has been utilized by hendrix, but after working on this project, I better see how much hendrix is supporting my major with the many facilities and computer lab.

### Issues
This experience has reshaped my social views of private universities. Before and during my time here at Hendrix, I thought it was like most other private universities. They are typically very expensive, have better education, and have better opportunities, but I wasn't sure if the price will ever be worth it. Tuition is so expensive, but im contributing to so many opportunities for all students and community members. If we didn't have these cruical donations, tuition would be more expensive and Hendrix wouldn't be as accessable. I have also gained a deeper understanding of why we should support the institutions we enjoy, because without a community supporting the institution, we wouldn't have the opportunities we enjoy.

### Conclusion
I feel like I have gained countless useful skills and ideas from this community project. I have worked with 3 of my peers, and a wonderful community partner. I feel like I have contributed a lot towards this project and Im excited to see the final product. I hope our work will make donations flow easier and take some of the burden off of Hendrix.
